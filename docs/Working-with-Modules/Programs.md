#  Programs Module

The **Programs** module is designed to support specialized patient care programs. It currently includes functionality for managing **Antenatal** and **Postnatal** services. These features help healthcare providers monitor maternal health before and after childbirth, ensuring continuity of care.



##  Antenatal Care 

The **Antenatal** tab focuses on tracking the health and progress of pregnant women during pregnancy. It ensures timely interventions, proper documentation, and consistent monitoring.

###  Features:

- **Patient Registration** for antenatal follow-up
- **Gestational Age Calculation** based on Last Menstrual Period (LMP)
- **Scheduled Visit Tracking** (ANC 1, ANC 2, ANC 3, ANC 4)
- **Vitals Monitoring**: Blood pressure, weight, fetal heartbeat
- **Laboratory Orders and Results** linked to ANC visits
- **Counseling and Education Notes**

##  Postnatal Care 

The **Postnatal** tab helps healthcare workers monitor the health of both mother and newborn after delivery. It covers essential follow-up care during the critical weeks following birth.

###  Features:

- **Postnatal Visit Scheduling** (e.g., PNC 1, PNC 2, PNC 3)
- **Mother's Condition Tracking**: Bleeding, uterine involution, breastfeeding
- **Newborn Assessment**: Weight, immunizations, reflexes
- **Documentation of Complications** and referrals if needed
- **Family Planning Counseling**

##  Integrated Patient Journey

Patients enrolled in Antenatal care can be seamlessly transitioned into the Postnatal program after delivery. This ensures continuity and completeness of maternal care records.

>  **Tip**: Always confirm delivery outcomes in the Antenatal tab to enable postnatal record linkage.
